    // CASE STUDY — 001

    E-Commerce Platform:
    Zero to 99.9% Uptime

    // DEPLOYMENT JOURNEY
    01
    The Problem
    A single VPS, manual SSH deploys, and zero monitoring — every release risked a 30-minute outage during business hours.
    02
    Architecture
    Mapped a layered, highly-available design: CDN edge, load-balanced app tier, and a replicated data layer with clear failure domains.
    03
    Infrastructure
    Provisioned a Proxmox HA cluster with ZFS storage pools, containerized every service, and codified it all with Ansible & Terraform.
    04
    Networking
    Nginx HA reverse proxy with SSL termination, rate limiting, and a Cloudflare edge for DDoS protection and global caching.
    05
    Deployment
    A GitOps CI/CD pipeline: push to main → tested, built, and rolled out with zero-downtime blue-green releases in under 90 seconds.
    06
    Monitoring
    Full observability with Prometheus, Grafana, and alerting rules — issues are caught and paged before users ever notice.
    07
    Scaling
    Auto-scaling with load balancing and hourly snapshots — traffic spikes are absorbed automatically and recovery is one tested command away.

    A growing e-commerce client was running a single VPS with manual deployments, no monitoring, and a deploy process that regularly caused 30-minute outages during business hours.

    Over 3 weeks, I re-architected their entire infrastructure: containerized all services, built a GitLab CI pipeline, deployed a Proxmox HA cluster, and set up full observability.

    Result: zero unplanned downtime in 8 months. Deploy time dropped from 45 minutes to 90 seconds.

    // OPERATING PRINCIPLES

    How I think about systems.

    Tools change; the discipline doesn't. A few principles guide every system I build and operate.

    01

    Automate what repeats.

    If I do it twice by hand, it becomes code. Toil is treated as a bug, not a routine.

    02

    Design for failure.

    Every system fails eventually. Mine fail safely, recover on their own, and page no one at 3 AM.

    03

    Observability first.

    You can't operate what you can't see. Metrics, logs, and traces ship before launch — never after.

    04

    Own the whole stack.

    From bare metal to TLS. No black boxes, no lock-in, no surprises when it matters most.
